# IB Academy Manifest

## Glossary

* Broker: a business entity that provides CFD and crypto trading services to traders.
* IB: a person whose main objective is to recruit new traders and to guide them through the onboarding funnel (from acquisition to first deposit).
* Academy: an IB hub whose primary purpose is to deliver educational content and empower IBs.

## Key objectives

* To achieve a 10% uplift in new trader acquisition attributable to Academy-produced content within 12 months among the participating IBs
* Achieve a 20% increase in exclusive partnership agreements among Academy-engaged IBs within 12 months, driven by the tangible value the Academy delivers to their business

## Problem statement

Currently, brokers provide IBs with dedicated IB areas that usually contain the following features:

* A dashboard showing IB earnings and KPIs
* Various reports (leads, rebates, IB networks)
* Referral links management system
* The means to contact the broker
* Some educational content (e.g., a simple FAQ)

However, all of this is not enough to improve IBs' effectiveness. IBs mostly rely on their own 'tribal' tools and knowledge to push leads along the funnel: offline and online events, personal communications, signal sharing, communications on social media, etc. As a result, a typical IB area fails to meaningfully empower an IB:

* There is no data available on the effectiveness of IBs' own tooling (such as information about social media engagement with an IB's posts)
* IBs lack the tooling to formulate their daily/weekly strategy in a structured manner, and mostly respond to 'ad-hoc' incidents such as new messages from leads or new event announcements
* There are no educational materials that dynamically adapt to reflect the tools IBs are using an IB's unique competencies (e.g., viral popularity on country-specific social media platforms)

Therefore, IBs mostly operate using outdated strategies and are often 'in the dark' about what they need to do to widen the onboarding funnel. The Academy project is meant to address the problems defined above.

## Solution

The IB Academy is a dedicated IB hub containing three key areas.

* 'Dashboard': a section containing stats about the key activities performed by the IB.
* 'Strategy': a section that uses natural language and visuals (e.g., diagrams) to propose what an IB should to within a specific timeframe (day, week, month, six months).
* 'Courses': a selection of written courses covering the basics of IB activities, as well as other topics including AI usage and country-specific issues.

Each area is designed to give IB the means to solve the three-part problem statement.

### Dashboard

The 'Dashboard' section presents several diagrams and figures of different formats (bar charts, pie charts, graphs, etc.) 

Possible examples of these diagrams include:

* Client churn by acquisition source
* Follower growth rate per channel (YouTube, Telegram, Instagram, X, TikTok, Discord)
* Engagement rate: likes, comments, shares and saves divided by reach
* Video watch time and average view duration, especially for market analysis or tutorials
* Telegram/WhatsApp channel growth, view-to-member ratio, and the share of members still active after 30 or 90 days

The exact metrics displayed in the dashboard are customizable and depend on an IB's profile. An IB should have two possible means to populate the dashboard with metrics.

* Using natural language. On first launch, an IB should be given a form with selectors allowing them to describe themselves and the tools they use. An AI tool will then select the most suitable dashboard preset for the IB.
* Using selectors. An IB should also have the option to choose a specific tool and the metrics available for that tool.

The ability to add custom metrics is planned but is not a priority. 

### Strategy

The 'Strategy' section generates and displays an overview of what an IB should ideally do within a specific timeframe (day, week, month, six months).

Using an AI tool, the section must do the following:

* Acquire information from the broker about the key IB metrics that are typically provided by the broker (e.g., Registration-to-fund rate, 90-day active trader retention, etc.).
* Acquire data from the dashboard based on its current configuration.
* Search the Internet for information about the possible activities that could be undertaken by the IB.
* Generate a text report with complementary visuals summarizing the acquired data and providing possible avenues for professional activities.

### Courses

The 'Courses' section contains structured tutorials covering the following topics.

* Core courses:
  * IB fundamentals
  * IB rewards and reward structures
  * Building multi-level IB networks
  * Key types of IB activities
  * Platform-specific advice
  * AI tooling advice

* IB-specific courses
  * Country-specific advice
